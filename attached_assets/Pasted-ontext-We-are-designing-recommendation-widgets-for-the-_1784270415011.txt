We are designing recommendation widgets for the e-commerce platform ShopNow. The focus domain should be Electronics (Mobile, Laptop, Accessories, etc.). The system must personalize recommendations for a known user persona (Rahul, 32 years old) while also handling anonymous users gracefully.

Requirements:

Recommendation Types:

Content-Based Filtering: Use product attributes (e.g., category, specs, color, brand) to recommend similar electronics.

Collaborative Filtering: Show items trending among similar shoppers, but avoid popularity bias.

Hybrid Filtering: Combine personalization with editorial curation to balance relevance and discovery.

AI/ML Design Considerations:

Implement color filtering (e.g., recommending accessories matching device colors).

Use editorial filtering to highlight curated collections (e.g., “Top Laptops for Students”).

Avoid cold start problems by using editorial loops and fallback strategies (e.g., trending electronics when user history is sparse).

Explicitly mitigate popularity bias so niche but relevant products still surface.

User Persona (Rahul, 32):

Recently purchased Nike Running Shoes (sports domain).

For electronics, assume Rahul is browsing laptops and mobile accessories.

Recommendations should adapt to Rahul’s browsing and purchase history, showing “Complete Your Electronics Kit” style AI cards.

AI Card Examples:

Personalized Card: “Rahul is upgrading his laptop for work. Suggested bundle: Laptop + Wireless Mouse + Laptop Sleeve.”

Content-Based Card: “Complete Your Mobile Setup: Phone + Protective Case + Fast Charger.”

Collaborative Card: “Frequently Bought Together: Laptop + External SSD.”

Hybrid Card: “Recommended For You: Curated mix of trending laptops and Rahul’s preferred accessories.”

Anonymous User Experience:

Homepage should default to editorial + popularity-based widgets (e.g., “Top Trending Mobiles,” “Best Laptop Deals”).

PDP (Product Detail Page) should show Frequently Bought Together and Trending Accessories.

Cart Page should highlight Cross-Sell Opportunities (e.g., chargers, headphones).

No personal AI cards, but still engaging through curated collections.
